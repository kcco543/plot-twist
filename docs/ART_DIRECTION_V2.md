# PLOT TWIST! art direction v2: "Lamplight"

> A living pixel storybook: atmospheric environments, expressive characters and a refined editorial interface.

**Status: Checkpoint 1, a proposal for review.** This document and the concept page in `docs/art-direction-v2/` change no application code. Nothing in `src/` should change until this checkpoint is approved.

Eastward, In Stars and Time, Until Then, SIGNALIS and NORCO are references for **qualities only**: environmental craft and lighting, expressive portraits, natural proportions, information hierarchy and environmental storytelling. No characters, assets, layouts or exact looks are copied. The palette, characters, rooms and UI here are original to PLOT TWIST!.

The concept page (`docs/art-direction-v2/lamplight-concept.html`) is an interactive version of everything below. It has one playable Play scene with Mira, Wren and Tobi, the six-expression sheet, the emotion glyph set, and sketches of the homepage, Character Studio and World Builder. All of its art is drawn in code, so it is also a working prototype of the light layer described in §5.

## 1. Audit

### 1.1 What makes the game read young

| Area | Today | Why it reads young | Lamplight answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Colour | Six pastel ramps (`lavender`, `pink`, `butter`, `mint`, `periwinkle`, `peach`) shared by the page, the world and every button. | There are no darks and no quiet colours, so nothing gets emphasis. Everything is equally bright and equally sweet. | Warm charcoal UI, one terracotta action colour, and world palettes that change with time of day. |
| Characters | 32×32 sprites with a figure about 28 px tall and a head about 12 px (about 2 heads). Dot eyes, blush, and a face shared across the cast. | Chibi proportions. The characters differ mostly by hair colour, and an 8 px face can't carry emotion. | About 2.7 heads in a taller cell, a silhouette hook per character, moving brows and mouth, and 48 px portraits with six expressions. |
| Light | Even, shadowless light. The Otherworld room has no light source. Generated maps (the bookshop) are richer but still flat midday. | Without light direction or a time of day, a room has no mood. | A runtime light layer: ambient light plus 2–4 named sources, quantised into hard pixel bands. |
| Type | Pixelify Sans, Press Start 2P and Silkscreen set the dialogue, body, buttons and labels. ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou sets Chinese display text. | Long lines in pixel type are tiring, and the Chinese face reads like a different game. | A literary serif for dialogue and titles, a clean sans for UI and a mono for labels. Pixels stay in the world. |
| Frames | 9-slice candy frames on every panel and button (`frames.css`), plus sparkles, kicker pills, scalloped bars and bobbing icons. | Every element wears the same ornament, so the eye has nowhere to land. Four equal candy buttons give no first action. | 1 px hairlines and a 3 px chamfer. Gold only marks focus and selection, and each view has one filled primary action. |
| Dialogue | A large narrator box beside the stage, mostly empty. No portrait. Emotion is a small effect over the head. Relationship changes are in the Story log only. | Conversation has no faces and no stakes on screen. | Two modes: ambient bubbles for autonomous chatter, and a focused panel with portrait, mood and relationship cues. |

### 2.2 Typography

| Role | Latin | Chinese | Size / leading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Logo, display | Newsreader Italic 600 | Noto Serif SC 600 | 48–72 |
| Screen and section titles | Newsreader 500 | Noto Serif SC 500 | 28–40 / 1.15 |
| Dialogue | Newsreader 400 | Noto Serif SC 500 | 19–21 / 1.45 (zh 1.6) |
| Moods ("Bristling") | Newsreader Italic 500 | Noto Serif SC 500 | 14–16 |
| Interface: buttons, body, fields | Instrument Sans 400–600 | Noto Sans SC 400–500 | 14–16, never under 16 in inputs on phones |
| Names, labels, status | IBM Plex Mono 500, caps, +0.12em | Noto Sans SC 500, no tracking | 11–12 |
| In-world text | Pixel glyphs drawn on the art grid | — | — |

Pixel fonts leave long text, forms and editors. Silkscreen may stay for one or two decorative stamps (for example a chapter card), never for running text. Every stack lists the Latin face first, then the CJK face, then a system fallback. CJK faces load by unicode range, as today.

### 2.3 Spacing and shape

- Keep the 4 px spacing scale, the `--panel-pad-*` layout tokens and the 44 / 48 / 56 px control heights.
- Corners are a **3 px chamfer** (`clip-path`), 6 px on large panels. No radius and no 9-slice candy frames.
- Borders are 1 px hairlines: `rgba(240,223,197,.14)` for quiet and `.26` for active. Focus is a 2 px gold outline with 2 px offset.
- Shadows are only used where a panel floats over the game art (dialogue, tooltips). Elsewhere, panels separate by tone.

### 2.4 Motion

Stepped timing stays (`steps()`), but there is less of it. Remove twinkles, bobbing icons and blinking "press start". Keep: the screen-in (240 ms, 4 steps), the modal pop, a typewriter for dialogue (skippable, off with reduced motion), and the emote pop (3 frames). Ambient world animation (flame, steam, dust, breathing) runs at about 12 fps on its own canvas.

## 3. Characters

### 3.1 Proportions

| | v1 | v2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprite cell | 32 × 32 | 18 × 36 (the canvas can grow to 32 × 40 for props and poses) |
| Figure height | about 28 px | 30–32 px |
| Head | about 12–14 px, about 2 heads | 12 px, about 2.7 heads |
| Face | dot eyes, blush | 1×2 eyes, 2 px brows that move, a 2–4 px mouth with 5 shapes |
| Outline | fixed `#5b4a86` | 1 px, generated from each edge pixel's own colour (darkened, shifted violet) |
| Portrait | 48 grid, one face | 48 grid, six expressions |

Arms, torso and legs are separated by colour and a 1 px shadow line. Clothing has at least two layers (for example a shirt under a cardigan, or a scarf over a coat).

Existing 32×32 sprites keep rendering. They are drawn with feet on the same baseline, so mixed casts still line up.

### 3.3 Expressions

Six faces are shared by sprite and portrait: **neutral, smile, angry, worried, surprised, shy**. The 11 engine emotions map onto them:

| Emotion | Face | Glyph |
| --- | --- | --- |
| happy, excited | smile | spark (excited only) |
| affectionate | shy | heart |
| embarrassed | shy | "…" |
| angry, frustrated | angry | vein |
| sad | worried | none (the face carries it) |
| anxious | worried | sweat drop |
| surprised | surprised | "!" |
| confused | worried | swirl |
| (curiosity, not an engine emotion yet) | neutral, head turn | "?", shown when a character inspects an object or asks a question |

Effect *size* still follows `EmotionLevel`: at level 1 the face only, at level 2 the face and glyph, at level 3 the face, glyph and a 1 px hop.

### 3.4 Emotion glyphs

- A glyph is drawn on the art grid in an 11 × 10 ivory bubble with a 1 px dark outline, placed up and to the right of the head. The sweat drop has no bubble.
- One glyph per character at a time. There are no particle showers, glows or emoji.
- The glyph pops in over 3 frames, then bobs 1 px every 1 s. Reduced motion shows it standing still.

### 3.5 Animation

- Idle breathing: the upper body moves 1 px every 1.1 s, with characters out of phase.
- Blink every 3–5 s (2 frames). Head turns move the eyes and face 1 px.
- Hesitation is a 2-frame lean back. Surprise is a 1 px hop and the "!" glyph.
- Walking keeps the existing path and collision code (`src/lib/stage/*`). Only the frames change.

## 5. The light layer

Lighting is a compositing layer, not baked art. That keeps the underlying pixel art readable and lets existing and generated maps gain atmosphere.

- **Model.** `colour = base × (ambient + Σ lightᵢ × bandᵢ)`. Each light has a position, an elliptical reach, a colour, a strength and optional flicker.
- **Pixel-honest falloff.** Each light's falloff is quantised into 4 hard bands, with a 2 × 2 ordered dither only in the middle 40% of each band seam. There is no blur and no smooth gradient.
- **Emissive pixels** (sky, flames, glass, screens) are never darkened.
- **Actors** keep at least 40% of their base brightness, so characters never vanish in shadow.
- **Shadow floor.** Dark pixels are lifted toward blue-violet. Nothing goes below `#1C1B21`.
- **Data.** Optional `timeOfDay` and `lights[]` on a scene. When they're absent, the scene renders as "Day, no lights", which is exactly today's look.
- **Cost.** One full-stage pass at about 12 fps. Light maps are precomputed, and only flicker is recomputed per frame.

## 7. Screens (to be built after approval)

- **Play** (Checkpoint 2): the environment takes most of the screen. The action row has Talk as primary, plus Observe, Wait and Twist. Cast, relationships and the inspected object sit beside or below the stage. Click-to-talk, drag-to-interact, autonomy, Story log and saves are unchanged.

- **Character Studio** (Checkpoint 3): the preview on the left stays visible while editing and shows the real saved sprite, portrait and expressions. The editor on the right has tabs, paper fields for long text, compact scales and relationships, and an Advanced disclosure. Every field still saves.
- **World Builder** (Checkpoint 3): a left rail (Map, Objects, Lighting, Walkable, Layers, then Generate, Map Lab and Refine area), the live editable map in the centre, and a right inspector. Generation, Map Lab, collision, refinement and export keep working.
